    Actually making this site proper is something that I have put off for months. This post serves as a way to state the intent for this site as well as a small introduction. 

    Why this site, exactly?

    I believe that it is important for anyone, especially anyone technical, to have some form of creative outlet. I have my own, of course, but in an experiment that I thought of a while back, I had the idea to take up some form of writing as a means to express myself better and to release my thoughts into the void of the internet. In the process of doing so I wish to explore and analyze various interesting subjects across my various interests and potentially also improve my writing skills. Communication is important! 

    Due to the nature of this project and what it needed, I chose to just use Publii to create this site instead of coding an entire one myself. It's a static site generator which is decently malleable and easy to use, recommended to me by a friend of mine in the games industry. I also just did not want to bother with the frontend or anything else of the sort for the site. I believe that if writing is the main focus, then there is no point in overkill website design.

    Ok, but why didn't you just use Claude to make the frontend/backend then? Sounds lazy to me.

    Good point. It feels slightly out of scope for this project, though. It certainly has its place as a development tool and a means to automate a lot of tasks (such as making a wesite for this blog very quickly) but I want full agency of my projects. I also believe that taking the easy way out and spending some tokens is not the way to properly learn something, reinforcing the fact that it feels out of scope. 

    Who are you anyway?

    How impolite of me. I'm Marcel, a Computer Science student at Carleton University. If you're in the CCSS, you've probably seen me around. If not or you don't know me, then nice to meet you!
